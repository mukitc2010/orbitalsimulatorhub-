## Content Guidelines

### For each post:
1. **Internal links:** Link to simulator, formulas, orbital mechanics, calculators, related blog posts.
2. **FAQ schema:** Add 2–4 FAQ items at the end for featured snippets.
3. **Data:** Use real numbers (ISS altitude, orbital speed, etc.) with sources.
4. **CTA:** End with "Try our [orbital simulator](/simulator)" or "Calculate with our [orbit calculator](/calculators).

### SEO best practices:
- **Title:** Include primary keyword in first 60 characters.
- **Meta description:** 150–160 chars, include keyword.
- **H2/H3:** Use question-style headings (e.g., "How fast does the ISS travel?").
- **Word count:** 800–1,500 for Tier 1; 600–1,000 for Tier 2–3.

### Backlink outreach:
- **Teachers:** "Free resource for your orbital mechanics unit."
- **Space forums:** "We built a tool for this — here's a guide."
- **University blogs:** "We have NASA-style data for aerospace students."
- **Wikipedia:** Some articles may cite your data (e.g., ISS altitude, orbital speed).
