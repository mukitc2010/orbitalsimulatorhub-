## 🔧 SEO Best Practices Applied

- **Title & meta:** Include main keywords; unique per page where possible.
- **Headings:** H1 = target keyword; H2/H3 = long-tail (e.g. "Why do satellites not fall?").
- **Length:** Learning and formula pages are long-form (good for Google).
- **Internal links:** Simulator, AstroBot, formulas, satellites, calculators link to each other.
- **External authority:** Links to NASA, JPL; cited constants and sources.
