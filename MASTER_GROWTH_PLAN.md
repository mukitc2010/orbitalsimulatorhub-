# PART 3 — VIRAL CONTENT SYSTEM

## Viral Content Engine

- **Format:** Short, shareable, mind-blowing
- **Examples:**
  - "The ISS travels at 17,500 mph—you could circle Earth in 90 minutes."
  - "A teaspoon of neutron star weighs 6 billion tons."
  - "There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on Earth."
- **Distribution:** Social cards, Pinterest, Instagram, Twitter/X
- **Goal:** 10–50 facts per month, each shareable

  - Neuro-space cognition (already on site)
