# References

## Active inference and scientific methodology

13. K. Friston, “The Free-Energy Principle,” 2010.

## Citation practice

For each future chapter expansion, cite primary mission documents, peer-reviewed experiments, dataset provenance, and the exact assumptions of simulations. Separate evidence, interpretation, and speculative proposals.
